Behind the designs

I'm Jerro Magano, a graphic designer from Quezon City, Philippines. I express my creativity through graphic design, digital art, and visual experimentation.

My creative direction is heavily influenced by streetwear culture, contemporary fashion, typography, and modern visual identities. I enjoy exploring different design techniques and developing graphics that communicate a distinct aesthetic.

I create designs for clothing brands in the Philippines and internationally, including markets such as the United States and Europe.

I believe every design should have its own identity. Whether it's typography, apparel graphics, or digital compositions, I aim to create visuals that are expressive, intentional, and memorable.

The creative process

  1. Experiment with different visual styles, references, and creative directions.

  2. Transform inspiration and ideas into a clear visual concept.

  3. Develop graphics through composition, typography, and digital design tools.

  4. Improve every detail until the final visual communicates the intended identity.
